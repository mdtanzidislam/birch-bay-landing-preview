How the resort is valued

A resort is both a home and a business. Its value comes from three parts.

1 Real property

The land and buildings, valued by the tax assessment. The 2020 tax value is in excess of $1 million.

2 Business assets

Almost every piece of furniture and all the equipment has been replaced during the current owners’ time, including the furniture and fixtures in every cabin. Rental boats, motors and hundreds of feet of dock are included.

3 The business itself

About 90% repeat business each year. Cabin rentals bring in about 95% of revenue; boat and motor rentals make up most of the rest, with a small share from clothing sales.

Our story

Over 70 years as a resort, one family since 1979

Ron and Bev Hemmingson visited the McKees, friends from Illinois who owned the resort, and learned they were ready to retire. They bought Birch Bay in 1979, and the resort, in existence for over 70 years, has been owned and operated by the same family ever since. Lorrie and Jeff have run, repaired and remodeled it since they met in 1983, and they would love to see it stay an active resort.
