This policy explains how Birch Bay Resort (“we”, “us”) collects and uses information when you visit this website or send us an inquiry about the sale of the resort at 30711 Zims Lane, Grand Rapids, Minnesota.

Information we collect

When you use the inquiry form or contact us, we collect the information you choose to give us: your name, email address, phone number, the type of buyer you are and any message you write.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. Information may be processed by the service providers that run this website, its forms and its analytics, only so they can provide those services to us, or disclosed if required by law.

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information you have shared with us, subject to applicable law. Contact us using the details below.
